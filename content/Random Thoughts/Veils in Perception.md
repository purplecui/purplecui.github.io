July 29, 2025

Today, like most evenings and some afternoons, i was sitting on my balcony resting on the dilapidated but comfortable chair that has survived many rains, storms and sunlight, i suddenly had a realization. I felt i was seeing myself from outside the veil of my perception, a vision of third person camera moment just for a momentary period. The space between myself and the point of observation felt like it was separated from minute veils of space full of minute silk threads but instead of silk it was translucent luminous strings. And in an instant a thought came to me. Oh how bland i seemed to myself, or rather form a third perspective. Before, I was there  gazing at a perching `sarau` on the rooftop tank of my neighbor, many thoughts racing thorough my mind. And then they shifted to flowers before me, on the balcony thinking of their momentary existence, their significance and what calmness they provide me thorough their happenings. Then when i had the moment of third camera perception, it all look bland. Just me, sitting there gazing towards things here and there; a speck of dust in cosmic timeline trying to understand greater timelines and seeking meaning of momentary existence. All thoughts racing thorough me, all things i was pondering, i couldn't see any else but a mere body of flesh from others' eyes. i felt how one could overlook others, their soul isn't perceivable. How in daily passing by multitudes of other people, in streets, in public and private space, even someone you spend most of your time with, even your closest friend; you have this image of them that is applicable only to you. Many unique images we carry to every other person, maybe a different person to every one. 

So how does it generates or rather how do we form these images. Or is it the person that appears who has more stake in formation of it or the person seeing them who has been thorough his own unique upbringing environment that has more stake. maybe it is a bit of both or one aspect weighs heavy on another. Or similar to the meaning portrayed by "a book read by thousand souls are thousand different books". As the great ones said, you can only know someone with the same depth that you have known of yourself.  But does shallowness has anything to do with it, as we being the beings of consciousness, our existence itself is the depth in evolutionary timeline, reinforced by us being the only species of human to outlast all others that existed. So how can we really know if the image of the person we see form the vantage point of our perception is the true one. But maybe we in ourselves are a cluster of many such near infinite images that the whole collective is the one that defines our being. A cluster that is ever-changing but also constant. A duality of sorts, perhaps the most important of intangible duality that exists. then comes the real question of important: are we ourselves see oneself as a single image, a single being or the accumulation of the components of cluster. And how it affects us in knowing, discovering, understanding and accepting plural clusters in comparison to knowing only a singular one, only perceiving but vaguely and unconfirmed or perceiving but not accepting their existence. How different are those who have accepted their depth in contrast who know nothing of them. What character, what greatness they might have achieved in character. Or what abyss hunts them. And perhaps it is also true when people say of soulmates, or of familiarity or matching vibes they think of the image of others that resonates with their self-image the most. Or again as the great ones said, one can only truly love someone when they know of their demons, or in this case some brief glimpse of their some of many images that exists.
