
## 1. Conceptual Understanding

Monetary policy controls money supply and interest rates through central banks.

---

## 2. Tools

- Interest rates
- Open market operations
- Reserve requirements

---

## 3. Intuition

Lower interest rates:
- Encourage borrowing
- Increase investment
- Stimulate economy

---

## 4. R Simulation

```r
interest_rate <- 5
new_rate <- 3

change <- interest_rate - new_rate
change
```

## 5. Real-World Example

- Federal Reserve rate cuts
- Inflation targeting policies

---

## 6. Interpretation Questions

- Why do central banks target inflation?
- How does interest rate affect GDP?
- What is liquidity trap?

---

## 7. Key Insight

Monetary policy works by influencing **borrowing and spending behavior in the economy**.
