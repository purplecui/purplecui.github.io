

## 1. Conceptual Understanding

Fiscal policy refers to government use of:
- Taxation
- Public spending

to influence the economy.

---

## 2. Types

### Expansionary Policy
- Increase spending
- Reduce taxes

### Contractionary Policy
- Reduce spending
- Increase taxes

---

## 3. R Simulation

```r
G_initial <- 200
G_new <- 250

fiscal_impact <- G_new - G_initial
fiscal_impact
```
