

## 1. Conceptual Understanding

Inflation refers to the sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services over time.

---

## 2. Intuition

When inflation occurs:
- Money loses purchasing power
- Prices increase across the economy

---

## 3. Causes of Inflation

### Demand-Pull Inflation
Too much demand in the economy

### Cost-Push Inflation
Increased production costs

---

## 4. R Simulation (Simple Inflation Calculation)

```r
price_t1 <- 100
price_t2 <- 110

inflation_rate <- ((price_t2 - price_t1) / price_t1) * 100
inflation_rate
```

## 5. Real-World Example

- Rising food prices
- Increasing fuel costs
- Wage-price spirals

---

## 6. Policy Implications

Central banks respond using:

- Interest rate changes
- Monetary tightening

- Why is moderate inflation considered healthy?

## 8. Key Insight

Inflation is not just price increase—it is a **monetary phenomenon affecting real purchasing power**.
