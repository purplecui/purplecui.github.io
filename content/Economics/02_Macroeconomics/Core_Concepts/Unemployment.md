
## 1. Conceptual Understanding

Unemployment refers to individuals who are actively seeking work but cannot find employment.

---

## 2. Types of Unemployment

- Frictional (job transitions)
- Structural (skill mismatch)
- Cyclical (recessions)

---

## 3. Intuition

Unemployment exists even in healthy economies due to:
- Search time
- Skill mismatches
- Economic fluctuations

---

## 4. R Calculation Example

```r
labor_force <- 1000
unemployed <- 80

unemployment_rate <- (unemployed / labor_force) * 100
unemployment_rate
```

## 5. Real-World Insight

- Tech layoffs → cyclical unemployment
- Automation → structural unemployment
