# Gross Domestic Product (GDP)

## 1. Conceptual Understanding

GDP measures the total value of all final goods and services produced within a country during a specific time period.

It is the primary indicator of economic activity.

---

## 2. Intuition

GDP answers:
> “How much is the economy producing?”

Example:
- If factories produce more goods
- If services increase
→ GDP rises

---

## 3. Methods of Calculation

### (1) Production Approach
Sum of value added across sectors

### (2) Income Approach
Sum of wages, rent, profit, interest

### (3) Expenditure Approach
GDP = C + I + G + (X - M)

---

## 4. R Implementation (Simple GDP Model)

```r
C <- 500   # Consumption
I <- 200   # Investment
G <- 300   # Government spending
X <- 150   # Exports
M <- 100   # Imports

GDP <- C + I + G + (X - M)
GDP
```

## 5. Real-World Interpretation

- Rising GDP → economic expansion
- Falling GDP → recession
- Negative growth → contraction

---

## 6. Interpretation Questions

- Why does GDP not measure inequality?
- Why are informal economies excluded?
- Can GDP increase while welfare decreases?

GDP measures **production, not wellbeing**.
