
# Aggregate Demand – Aggregate Supply Model

## 1. Conceptual Understanding

The AD-AS model explains how total demand and total supply determine:
- Output (GDP)
- Price level

---

## 2. Aggregate Demand (AD)

Represents total demand in economy:
- Consumption
- Investment
- Government spending
- Net exports

---

## 3. Aggregate Supply (AS)

Represents total production capacity of economy.

---

## 4. R Simulation (Conceptual Shift Model)

```r
AD_shift <- 100
AS_level <- 100

equilibrium_output <- AD_shift + AS_level
equilibrium_output
```

## 5. Real-World Application

- COVID-19 → AS shock
- Stimulus spending → AD shift

---

## 6. Interpretation Questions

- What happens when AD increases?
- How does AS shock affect inflation?
- Why do economies self-correct in long run?

---

## 7. Key Insight

AD-AS explains **short-run fluctuations and long-run equilibrium**.
