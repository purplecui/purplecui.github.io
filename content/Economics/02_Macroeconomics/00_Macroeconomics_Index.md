# Macroeconomics — Complete Study Framework

## 1. Overview

Macroeconomics studies the behavior of the economy as a whole. Unlike microeconomics, which focuses on individual agents, macroeconomics focuses on aggregate outcomes such as total output, inflation, employment, and economic growth.

It provides the theoretical foundation for understanding:
- National income
- Business cycles
- Government policy
- Financial stability

## 5. Study Method

Each topic should be studied in five steps:

1. Intuition (what is happening in the economy)
2. Theory (formal definitions)
3. Model (AD-AS / IS-LM)
4. Data (R-based computation)
5. Policy interpretation

---

## 6. Key Insight

Macroeconomics is fundamentally the study of:
> How millions of individual decisions aggregate into national-level outcomes.
