
## Overview

Microeconomics is the branch of economics that studies how individuals, households, and firms make decisions under conditions of scarcity. It explains how resources are allocated through markets and how prices coordinate economic activity.

This vault is structured to function as a complete learning system combining:

- Theoretical foundations (lecture-style explanations)
- Mathematical reasoning (derivations and models)
- Computational implementation (R-based simulations)
- Applied economics (case studies and real-world interpretation)
